My Gym Schedule

A simple split to stay active, strong, and consistent.

Here is my simple workout schedule that helps me stay active, strong, and consistent. I know it can be hard to be healthy as a college student, this plan is designed to help me be constistent, despite how hectic my schedule can be.

How my week is built

💪

Strength

Leg day and Back & Biceps day are my main lifting sessions. They build muscle and keep me progressing.

🧘

Flexibility & Balance

Pilates gives me a lower-impact day focused on core strength, mobility, and controlled movement.

🏃

Endurance

Cardio keeps my stamina up and keeps me moving on the days I'm not lifting.

Legs

My leg day focuses on building strength in my legs and improving lower-body fitness. I start with the heavier compound lifts, then finish with isolation work.

Workout

  • Hip Thrust
  • Leg Press
  • Romanian Deadlifts
  • Leg Extensions
  • Hamstring Curls

Upper Body & Core

Back & Biceps

This workout focuses on strengthening my back and building my biceps. Pulling movements also help my posture after long hours of studying.

  • Lat Pulldowns
  • Seated Rows
  • Pull-Ups
  • Bicep Curls
  • Hammer Curls

Pilates

Pilates helps me focus on core strength, flexibility, balance, and controlled movement. It's a good way to recover while still staying active.

  • Core exercises
  • Leg exercises
  • Stretching
  • Balance exercises

Cardio

My cardio workouts help improve my endurance and keep me active throughout the week. I mix up the machines so it never gets boring.

Workout

  • Treadmill
  • StairMaster
  • Incline Walking
  • Running

Consistency beats perfection

I don't need a perfect week. I just need to show up, follow the split, and keep going.
